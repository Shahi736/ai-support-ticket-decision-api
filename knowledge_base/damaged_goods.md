# Damaged Goods Policy

## Reporting Window
Customers must report damaged or defective items within 7 days of delivery to be eligible for a replacement or refund.

## Photo Evidence Requirement
- Orders valued above ₹2,000 require photographic evidence of the damage before a replacement or refund is issued.
- Orders valued at ₹2,000 or below may be resolved without photo evidence, at support agent discretion.

## Resolution Options
Customers with a valid damage claim may choose between:
1. A free replacement of the same item (subject to stock availability)
2. A full refund
3. Store credit for 110% of the item's value

## Non-Qualifying Damage
Damage caused by customer misuse after delivery (e.g., dropped, water damage after unboxing) is not covered under this policy.

## Escalation
Claims involving damage to items valued over ₹10,000 must be escalated to a senior support agent and cannot be auto-resolved.
