# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Refunds are available for orders within 30 days of delivery. Orders older than 30 days are not eligible for a refund unless the item is defective (see Damaged Goods Policy).

## Refund Methods
- Orders paid by card or UPI are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
- Orders paid by cash on delivery are refunded via bank transfer; the customer must provide bank account details.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Gift cards
- Digital downloads
- Items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase

## Partial Refunds
If only part of an order is being returned, a partial refund is issued for the value of the returned items only, minus any applicable restocking fee (see Returns Policy).

## Refund Amount Thresholds
- Refunds under ₹2,000 are processed automatically after return confirmation.
- Refunds of ₹2,000 or more require manager approval before processing.
