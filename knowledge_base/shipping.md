# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Timeframes
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days.
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days.
- Orders placed after 3 PM are processed the next business day.

## Shipping Costs
- Free standard shipping on orders over ₹1,500.
- Orders under ₹1,500 incur a flat ₹100 shipping fee.
- Express shipping costs ₹250 regardless of order value.

## Delayed Shipments
If a shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window by more than 3 days, the customer is eligible for a shipping fee refund upon request.

## Lost Packages
Packages not delivered within 15 days of the estimated delivery date are considered lost. Customers should be offered a full refund or a reshipment at no additional cost.

## International Shipping
International orders may take 10-20 business days and are subject to customs fees not covered by the company.
